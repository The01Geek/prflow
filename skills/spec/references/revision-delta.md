### Revision-delta verification (shared procedure — referenced by every revise-and-re-gate site)

Every revise-and-re-gate sentence in this skill (Step 3.5's items 5 and 6, Step 3.6's `VERDICT: REVISE` handling and its user-chosen rounds, and Step 4 sub-step 4's two revision sentences) references this one procedure by name; it is stated here, once, and no site paraphrases it. It runs **at every revision event** — before any re-audit dispatch at that site and before any presentation of the revised draft. (A "revision event" is one revise-and-re-gate occurrence; a multi-finding `VERDICT: REVISE` is one event spanning several verify-and-edit batches.)

1. **Bind and walk the delta per edit-batch.** An edit-batch is the set of draft edits you apply in one assistant turn, so each batch's walk (point 2) runs in that same turn. A revision event spanning several turns accumulates one walk per batch, and the event's closing evidence line (point 7) sums them, reading them from their own transcript records. The walk runs at edit time, never retroactively.
2. Each batch walk covers all six delta classes, per class, recording what the batch introduces, changes, or removes in each — a removal counts: (a) mechanisms, (b) lifecycle rules (artifact and state lifecycles, ordering), (c) execution-tier assumptions, (d) dependencies (in-repo helper/resolver/gate/artifact reliances, and relied-on third-party behavior), (e) universal guarantees and quantifiers, and (f) the residual class — any other new load-bearing claim, file reference, value comparison, or delta content fitting none of (a)–(e). The walk record is transcript text: one compact entry per class — the class's enumerated items, else a stated falsifiable zero naming why the batch has none ("no new mechanism: the batch touches only the Testing Strategy's fixture names"), never a bare zero and never a single aggregate covering all six classes. Cite tool evidence inside a walk entry as a one-line summary, never pasted output.
3. An all-zeros walk ends the batch's work at the walk record and point 4's draft-consistency check. Wording fixes, citation additions, and scope narrowing enumerate to nothing only when the edit introduces no fact; a replacement that introduces a fact about current behavior, a universal claim, or an environment-dependent claim enumerates in its delta class (the residual class (f), the quantifier class (e), or the execution-tier class (c) respectively), never as a wording fix. Such an all-zeros batch costs the six one-clause zero entries, that check, and the summary line. There is no separate classify-then-skip gate: the per-class walk itself decides, and its stated zeros are the auditable record of that skip decision.
4. For each enumerated item, re-evaluate the premises and contracts triggers using Step 3.5 item 2's applicability rule; load a newly required group per *Reference routing* before re-applying its applicable rows with fresh reads. Verify each enumerated (non-empty-class) item with the delta-scoped reuse of the two existing Step 3.5 disciplines, referenced by discipline name, never copied as a list: the load-bearing-claim premise verification of Step 3.5's item 2 (value-comparison grounding in `references/quality-group-contracts.md`; conditional-path and value-semantics reading and the relied-on-third-party documentation ladder in `references/quality-group-premises.md` — these groups loaded and re-applied under item 2's routing; universal-quantifier and occurrence-count grounding in item 2 itself) and the unstated-mechanism-dependency sweep of Step 3.5's item 4 (with its existence-determination step and its stated-but-unbound-input binding clause) — via targeted tool reads against the repository scoped to the enumerated items, never ambient context. Every batch, an all-zeros one included, also runs the draft-consistency check: Step 3.5 item 3's projection and missed-edge-case checks plus the template's AC mutual-consistency rule it points to on each sentence the batch adds, changes or removes — an auditor's recommended edit, must-revise or advisory, the same as your own — and on each limit the batch accepts (a case its own mechanism knowingly leaves uncovered; a narrowing the user chose is decided behavior, not a limit), against the rest of the draft. A passing sentence narrows, widens or contradicts no Desired Behavior sentence or criterion, drops no exclusion another criterion states, and adds no obligation without an end condition; a passing limit is stated by every Desired Behavior sentence and criterion covering its case, and a failing sentence or limit is a draft-consistency conflict. The walk record lists each limit the batch accepts, else states a falsifiable zero, and names each sentence or limit checked and the sentences it was checked against by draft locator, not quotation. Otherwise the omissions hunt (item 3) and the Step 3.6 dimension self-check (item 6) stay one-shot whole-draft passes; this procedure reuses checks, it does not re-run Step 3.5.
5. Fix what it finds inline, re-run the Step 3 no-options gate, and walk the fix as its own edit-batch; a batch with an all-zeros walk and no draft-consistency conflict ends the loop. This is the same inline-revise shape as Step 3.5's item 5, and each successive batch covers only its own edits. Edit the brief as a document, applying the replacement/consolidation/deletion revision discipline stated once in `skills/spec/references/issue-template.md`'s brief/investigation-record routing rule (pointed at, not restated here).
6. Route a genuinely new unresolved decision fork through the existing Step 2 machinery (the runner's user-question tool; `## 🚫 Blocked` on disengagement) — add no new decision-handling path.
7. Close every revision event with one standalone evidence line, at the per-site anchor below: `revision-delta check: <N> enumerated, <V> verified, <F> fixed` (summing the event's batch walks) when any class in any batch was non-empty, or `revision-delta check: no verifiable delta` when every batch walked all-zeros. The line does not extend the Step 4 audit summary line. Persist it — in addition to reporting it in chat — by appending it to a `## Revision-delta record` section of this run's derivation artifact `.prflow/tmp/spec/<slug>/issue-derivation-<slug>.md` (the same artifact Step 3.5's record lives in), creating the section on the first line written and leaving the artifact's `## Evidence bundle` and `## Steelman record` sections untouched; each appended line names the anchor it came from (e.g. `revision-delta check: … [anchor: Step 3.5 item 5]`). On a read-only filesystem it degrades to a visible inline-in-chat block per `skills/spec/references/fallback-read-only-sandbox.md`'s per-artifact Revision-delta-record arm. No confirmation covers this record. Classified against the anchor list below, which is stated once and is this item's only enumeration of the anchors: only the first anchor (Step 3.5's item 5) fires before the Step 3.6 entry confirmation, its line being emitted wholly within Step 3.5, which completes before Step 3.6 is entered; the other four fire after it — including item 6's, whose listed timing lands after the entry confirmation, the first thing Step 3.6 does, even though the item is numbered under Step 3.5. Five anchors cover all six wired sites, because the two Step 4 sub-step 4 revision sentences share one: at Step 3.5's item 5, immediately after that item's gate re-run; at Step 3.5's item 6, immediately before the initial Step 3.6 dispatch's pre-dispatch draft write; at the Step 3.6 `VERDICT: REVISE` site, immediately before the re-audit dispatch; at a user-chosen round, immediately before the T1/T2 trigger re-evaluation and its offer message; at the Step 4 sub-step 4 sites, immediately before the re-presented draft.
