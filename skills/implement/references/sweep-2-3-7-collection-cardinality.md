#### 2.3.7 Collection-cardinality sweep (when the change adds ordering, deduplication or aggregation)

Check that the chosen verification exercises the collection behavior: multiple elements for ordering or combining values, duplicates for deduplication, and distinct values for a tie-breaker. Reuse an existing case when it covers the changed outcome. A pass-through collection needs no cardinality-specific case.

Apply §2.3's verification policy when choosing automation or a direct check, and §2.4 for model instructions. Record material gaps with the other verification evidence. This sweep adds no separate test quota or waiver.
