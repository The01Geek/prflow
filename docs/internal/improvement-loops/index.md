These pages explain the mechanisms that measure, calibrate, and improve PRFlow. They distinguish measured behavior from self-reports and unestablished claims.

- [Efficiency telemetry](efficiency-telemetry.md) — subagent effectiveness records and persistence.
- [Workflow flight recorder](workflow-flight-recorder-overview.md) — local workflow inventory and recovery evidence.
- [Calibration and adjudication](calibration-and-adjudication.md) — grading calibration and evidence.
- [Runtime evaluations](runtime-evaluations.md) — context, wording, and runtime studies.
- [Incidents and audits](incidents-and-audits.md) — outages, audits, and their current conclusions.

The weekly retrospective loop (`/prflow:retrospective-weekly`, `lib/scan.sh`, `lib/cheap-gate.jq`, `.prflow/learnings/`) has no dedicated page yet; `docs/internal/DEVFLOW_SYSTEM_OVERVIEW.md` §12 is its current reference.
